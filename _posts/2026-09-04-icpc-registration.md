# ICPC

The International Collegiate Programming Contest is an algorithmic programming contest for college students. Teams of three, representing their university, work to solve the most real-world problems, fostering collaboration, creativity, innovation, and the ability to perform under pressure. Through training and competition, teams challenge each other to raise the bar on the possible. Quite simply, it is the oldest, largest, and most prestigious programming contest in the world.

[To know more about ICPC](https://icpc.global/regionals/abouticpc){:target="_blank"}

## Structure

1. Online Preliminary Contest - Online mode
2. Onsite Regional Contest - Chennai, Amritapuri, Kanpur and Mathura
3. Onsite Regional Chmpionship - ICPC Asia West Championship
4. World Finals

## Registration

* Form your team consisting of three members from the same institution, one optional reserve contestant and one coach. 
* Preliminary round will be conducted on 3rd October 2026 in online mode and top teams from prelims will make it to onsite round
* Women teams have reserved slots for the on-sites
* Fee for Preliminary round is 1500 and for On-site regional round is 4500
* On-sites will be conducted in [Chennai](https://icpcchennai.vercel.app/){:target="_blank"}, [Amritapuri](https://amritaicpc.in/){:target="_blank"}, [Kanpur](https://kanpur.indiaicpc.in/){:target="_blank"} and [Mathura](https://mathuraicpc.in/){:target="_blank"}. Read the descriptions regarding the exact locations. Amritapuri regional will be conducted across four cities Kollam, Bengaluru, Coimbatore and Mysuru. Though the Amritapuri regional is spread across four cities, all these comes under Ampritapuri regional.
* Registration closes by **20th September, 5PM**. This is a strict timeline
* Read all the timelines and schedules carefully from the respective on-site ICPC regionals website

### Steps

1. Register your team at [ICPC Global](https://icpc.global/login){:target="_blank"}
2. Choose the region as Asia West and the on-site region you wish to contest. A team may register for at most two Indian regional sites, but registration and the preliminary-round fee must be completed separately for each site, and it's recommended you keep the same team members across sites. Only those teams that qualify the online preliminary contest will be invited to participate in the On-site contest based on the rank. Ranking and cut-off details will be revealed later by the respective regions
3. For further steps regarding submission of proof of payment, go through the registration process mentioned in the respective On-site websites mentioned above

### Eligibility

* A contestant is eligible if they first began post-secondary studies in 2022 or later, or were born in 2003 or later, per the ICPC Global eligibility rules
* All participants must be strictly from the same institution
* Read the official eligibility rules [here](https://icpc.global/regionals/rules){:target="_blank"}
