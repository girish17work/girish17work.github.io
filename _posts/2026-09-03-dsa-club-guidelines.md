DSA Club Guidelines

* The objective of the club is to practice DSA challenges daily with consistency
* Membership in the club is not compelled, its based on your choice, likewise, solving problem sets
* Asking for help in the GSpace about any live contests or live interviews is strictly prohibited. Strict actions will be taken against those who indulge in the same
* Live sessions will be conducted occasionally
* Problem sets will be given, members are expected to solve and document their learnings
* Understanding the problem, the concepts and the optimisations is far more important than simply solving the challenge
* Do not use AI to solve a challenge, use AI to understand the logic and to make optimisations. Avoid AI for implementations
* Start with brute force solution (getting TLE is fine) and then attempt to optimise that with proper intuitions or mathematical principles
* Quality of challenges and concepts value more than quantity
* You will be notified about contests across different platforms, try to take part and give your best. Cheating or using AI to solve a challenge does not make sense
* GSpace is strictly for discussion about DSA challenges. Harassing or promotion is strictly prohibited. The Space is monitored by the officials.
* Members are encouraged to maintain similar blogs to document their learning

This is a work in progress document. Guidelines will be updated frequently.
